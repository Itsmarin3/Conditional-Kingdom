ZERO CONDITIONAL

Used for universal facts, scientific rules, and general truths.

If + Present Simple, Present Simple

FIRST CONDITIONAL

Used for real or likely future possibilities and predictions.

If + Present Simple, Will + Verb
  • If I finish my homework, I will watch a movie.
  • If we leave early, we will arrive on time.
  • If you practice speaking, you will improve your English.
